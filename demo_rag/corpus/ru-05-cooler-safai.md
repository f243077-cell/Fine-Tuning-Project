# Hawa cooler ki safai

Garmiyon mein Hawa cooler ke cooling pads har do hafte saaf karein aur saal mein ek dafa badal dein. Hawa wale mausam mein tank ka pani har teen din baad nikal kar tank dho lein, warna badbu aati hai.

Cooler ko khuli khidki ya darwaze ke paas rakhein, kyun ke is ko taaza hawa chahiye. Band kamre mein cooling kam ho jati hai.
