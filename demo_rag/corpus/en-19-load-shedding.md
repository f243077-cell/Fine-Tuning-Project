# Products for load-shedding

Noorbagh designs its products for homes that face power cuts. The Sitara fan can run on a 12-volt battery with the DC inverter kit (Rs. 4,500). The Shams lantern charges from the sun and can top up a phone. The Barq power bank keeps phones and routers running.

A common setup for a small room is one Sitara fan on the DC kit, one Shams lantern, and one Barq power bank for a Wi-Fi router. With a 100 Ah battery, the fan alone runs for about 8 hours at medium speed.
