# Payment methods

Noorbagh accepts debit and credit cards (Visa and Mastercard), bank transfer, the two main mobile wallets, and cash on delivery.

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to Rs. 50,000. Orders above that amount must be paid in advance by card or bank transfer.

Instalment plans are offered with partner banks on orders above Rs. 20,000 for 3, 6, or 12 months. The customer's bank decides any mark-up on instalments; Noorbagh itself adds no fee.
