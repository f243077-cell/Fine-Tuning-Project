# Stores ke auqaat

Noorbagh ke teen company stores hain: Lahore mein 14 Canal View Road, Karachi mein Bahadurabad Market ki Shop 7, aur Islamabad mein F-10 Markaz.

Stores roz subah 11 baje se raat 10 baje tak khulte hain, sirf Jumma ko 3 baje khulte hain. Har store par service desk hai jahan warranty claim jama hota hai aur chhoti moti repair wahin ho jati hai.
