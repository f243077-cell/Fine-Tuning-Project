# Refund ki policy

Delivery ke 14 din ke andar agar product istemal nahi hua aur asal dabbe mein hai to poora refund milta hai. 14 se 30 din ke darmiyan cash refund ki bajaye store credit milta hai.

Card refund 5 se 7 kaam ke din mein aata hai aur bank transfer refund 3 din mein. Cash on delivery wale order ka refund bank transfer se hota hai, is liye support ko account title aur IBAN dena hota hai.
