# Company stores

Noorbagh has three company stores. The Lahore store is at 14 Canal View Road, next to the head office. The Karachi store is at Shop 7, Bahadurabad Market. The Islamabad store is in the F-10 Markaz.

All stores open from 11 am to 10 pm every day except Friday, when they open at 3 pm. Each store has a service desk that accepts warranty claims and does basic repairs while the customer waits.

Partner shops sell Noorbagh products but cannot process refunds; refunds go through the website or a company store.
