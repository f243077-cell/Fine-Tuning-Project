# Hawa air cooler troubleshooting

If the cooler blows air but does not cool, check that the water tank is filled above the minimum line and that the pump switch is on. Dry cooling pads mean the pump is not running.

A bad smell usually comes from old water: empty and clean the tank every three days in humid weather. If water leaks, check that the drain plug is closed and the tank is not overfilled.

If the fan does not start at all, check the fuse in the plug. If the problem continues, contact support for a service visit.
