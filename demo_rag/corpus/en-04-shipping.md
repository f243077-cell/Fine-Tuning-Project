# Shipping and delivery

Noorbagh delivers to all major cities in Pakistan. Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Delivery takes 2 to 3 working days in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad, and 3 to 6 working days elsewhere.

Delivery is free for orders above Rs. 5,000. Smaller orders pay a flat delivery charge of Rs. 250. Air coolers have a fixed delivery charge of Rs. 600 because of their size, whatever the order total.

Every order gets a tracking number by SMS. If a parcel has not moved for 5 days, customers should contact support with the tracking number.
