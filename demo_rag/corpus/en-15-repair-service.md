# Repair and service centers

Authorized Noorbagh service centers repair products in and out of warranty. Out-of-warranty repairs have a fixed inspection fee of Rs. 500, which is waived if the customer goes ahead with the repair.

Most fan and cooler repairs are finished within 5 working days. Spare parts are kept for at least 5 years after a model stops being sold.

For air coolers, a technician can visit the customer's home in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad for a visit charge of Rs. 800.
