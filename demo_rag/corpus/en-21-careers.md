# Working at Noorbagh

Noorbagh hires engineers, technicians, store staff, and customer support agents. Open positions are posted on the careers page.

The standard working week is 5 days for office staff and 6 days on a rotating roster for store staff. All employees get medical cover for themselves and their families, and an annual bonus based on company results.

New technicians follow a 3-month paid training program at the Lahore workshop before they work alone.
