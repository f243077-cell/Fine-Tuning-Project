# Product sheet: Barq power bank BP-20

The Barq BP-20 is a 20,000 mAh power bank with two USB-A ports and one USB-C port. The USB-C port supports 22.5-watt fast charging in both directions. A full recharge takes about 4 hours with a fast charger.

It weighs 410 grams and has a small display that shows the remaining charge as a percentage. It is allowed in cabin baggage on most airlines because it is under 100 watt-hours.

The list price is Rs. 6,800 and it has a 1-year warranty.
