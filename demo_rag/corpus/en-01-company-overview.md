# Noorbagh Electronics: company overview

Noorbagh Electronics is a fictional home-electronics company used only for this demo. It was founded in 2011 in Lahore by two engineers, Hina Qadir and Faraz Mir, and started by repairing ceiling fans in a small workshop on Ferozepur Road.

Today the company designs and sells five product lines: the Sitara ceiling fan, the Barq power bank, the Shams solar lantern, the Hawa air cooler, and the Chiragh LED bulb. Products are sold through the company website, three company stores (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad), and about 140 partner shops.

The head office is at 14 Canal View Road, Lahore. The company has about 320 employees. Its stated mission is "reliable power and comfort for every home, even during load-shedding".
