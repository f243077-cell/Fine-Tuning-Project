# Warranty terms

Every Noorbagh product has a manufacturer warranty that starts on the delivery date. The Sitara ceiling fan has a 2-year warranty on the motor and 1 year on the regulator. The Hawa air cooler has 1 year on the pump and motor. The Barq power bank and the Shams solar lantern have 1 year. Chiragh LED bulbs have 6 months.

The warranty covers manufacturing faults only. It does not cover damage from voltage spikes without a stabilizer, water damage on products that are not water-resistant, or repairs done by someone other than an authorized Noorbagh service center.

To claim the warranty, keep the invoice or the order number. The serial number on the product must match the invoice.
