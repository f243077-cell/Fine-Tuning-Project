# Product sheet: Sitara ceiling fan SF-56

The Sitara SF-56 is a 56-inch ceiling fan with a copper-wound motor. It uses 75 watts at full speed and 28 watts at the lowest of its five speeds. With the optional DC inverter kit it can run on a 12-volt battery during load-shedding.

The fan comes in white, brown, and silver. It is sold with a wall regulator; a remote control is an optional accessory that costs Rs. 1,200. The recommended minimum ceiling height is 9 feet.

The list price is Rs. 11,500. The motor has a 2-year warranty.
