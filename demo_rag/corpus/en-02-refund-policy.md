# Refund policy

Customers can ask for a full refund within 14 days of delivery if the product is unused and in its original box. After 14 days and up to 30 days, Noorbagh offers store credit instead of a cash refund.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 7 working days. Bank transfer refunds take 3 working days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer, so the customer must share an account title and IBAN with the support team.

Products that were opened and used are not refunded, but they may still be covered by the warranty. Delivery charges are refunded only when the product arrived damaged or was the wrong item.
