# Product sheet: Shams solar lantern SL-3

The Shams SL-3 is a solar lantern with a built-in 5-watt solar panel and a 4,000 mAh battery. On a full charge it gives up to 12 hours of light on low mode and 5 hours on high mode. It can also charge a phone through its USB port.

A full solar charge takes about 8 hours of direct sunlight. The lantern is splash-resistant (IPX4) but must not be left in rain.

The list price is Rs. 3,900. It has a 1-year warranty.
