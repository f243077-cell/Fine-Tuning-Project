# Pankhe ki awaz aur hilna

Agar Sitara pankha hil raha ho ya awaz kar raha ho to pehle teeno blades ke screws check karein. Dheele screws hilne ki sab se aam wajah hain. Screws barabar kasein aur blades par jami mitti saaf karein.

Agar phir bhi awaz aaye to pankhe ka hook check karein, jo kam az kam 30 kilo wazan utha sake. Masla hal na ho to service center se rabta karein; warranty ke andar repair muft hai.
