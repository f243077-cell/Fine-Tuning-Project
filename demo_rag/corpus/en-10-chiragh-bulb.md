# Product sheet: Chiragh LED bulb LB-12

The Chiragh LB-12 is a 12-watt LED bulb that gives 1,100 lumens, about the same as a 100-watt filament bulb. It comes in warm white (3000 K) and cool daylight (6500 K) and fits a standard B22 holder.

The rated life is 15,000 hours. The bulb works on 150 to 260 volts, which helps in areas with low voltage.

A single bulb costs Rs. 450 and a pack of four costs Rs. 1,600. The warranty is 6 months.
