# Company history

2011: Hina Qadir and Faraz Mir open a fan repair workshop in Lahore.
2014: The first Sitara fan is sold.
2017: The Karachi store opens and the Shams solar lantern is launched.
2019: The Barq power bank is launched.
2021: The Islamabad store opens.
2023: The Hawa air cooler and the Chiragh LED bulb are launched, and the company reaches 300 employees.
