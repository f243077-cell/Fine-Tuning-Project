# Noorbagh Points loyalty program

Customers earn 1 Noorbagh Point for every Rs. 100 spent. Each point is worth Rs. 1 on a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

Members reach Gold tier after spending Rs. 60,000 in a calendar year. Gold members get free delivery on every order, including air coolers, and an extra 6 months of warranty on Sitara fans.

Points cannot be exchanged for cash and are not earned on delivery charges.
