# Energy-saving tips

Running a fan at medium speed instead of full speed saves about 40% of its power. Replacing a 100-watt filament bulb with a Chiragh LB-12 saves 88 watts per bulb.

Clean fan blades every month; dust on the blades makes the motor work harder. Keep the air cooler near an open window or door so it gets fresh air. Switch off chargers at the wall when they are not in use.
