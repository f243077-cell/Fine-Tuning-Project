# Warranty claim kaise karein

Warranty claim ke liye apni invoice ya order number sambhal kar rakhein. Product par likha serial number invoice se milna chahiye.

Sitara pankhe ki motor par 2 saal ki warranty hai aur regulator par 1 saal. Hawa cooler, Barq power bank aur Shams lantern par 1 saal, aur Chiragh bulb par 6 mahine ki warranty hai.

Warranty sirf factory ki kharabi ko cover karti hai. Stabilizer ke baghair voltage ki wajah se kharabi, ya kisi ghair-authorized mistri se repair karwane par warranty khatam ho jati hai.
