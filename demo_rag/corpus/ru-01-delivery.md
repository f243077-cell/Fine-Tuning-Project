# Delivery ki maloomat

Noorbagh Pakistan ke tamam bare shehron mein delivery karta hai. Agar order kaam ke din dopehar 2 baje se pehle diya jaye to usi din dispatch ho jata hai. Lahore, Karachi aur Islamabad mein delivery 2 se 3 din mein hoti hai, baaqi shehron mein 3 se 6 din.

Rs. 5,000 se zyada ke order par delivery muft hai. Is se chhote order par Rs. 250 delivery charge lagta hai. Air cooler ki delivery hamesha Rs. 600 hai.

Har order ka tracking number SMS par aata hai. Agar parcel 5 din tak aage na barhe to tracking number ke sath support se rabta karein.
