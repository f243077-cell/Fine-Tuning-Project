# Installing the Sitara ceiling fan

Turn off the main breaker before installing a ceiling fan. The fan hook must hold at least 30 kilograms. Fix the down-rod to the motor, hang the fan on the hook, and connect the live wire through the regulator.

Fit the three blades with the screws supplied and tighten them evenly; loose screws cause wobbling. The blade tips should be at least 7 feet above the floor.
