# Customer support contact

The customer support team works from 9 am to 9 pm, Monday to Saturday, and from 11 am to 5 pm on Sunday. The team answers in English and Urdu.

Customers can reach support through the website chat, by email at support@noorbagh.example, or on the support line. The average first reply time on chat is under 10 minutes during working hours.

For a faster answer, keep the order number ready. Support staff never ask for a card PIN, a one-time password, or an account password.
