# Power bank safety

Do not leave the Barq power bank in a parked car in summer; temperatures above 45 degrees Celsius can damage the battery. Stop using it if it swells, gets very hot, or smells of burning.

Use a charger rated 5 to 12 volts. Do not cover the power bank with a pillow or blanket while it charges.

Old or damaged power banks should not go in household waste. Any company store accepts them for recycling for free.
