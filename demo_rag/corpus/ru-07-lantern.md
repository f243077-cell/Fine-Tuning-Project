# Shams solar lantern ka istemal

Shams SL-3 lantern ko poora charge karne ke liye taqreeban 8 ghante ki seedhi dhoop chahiye. Poore charge par low mode mein 12 ghante aur high mode mein 5 ghante roshni deta hai.

Is ke USB port se phone bhi charge ho sakta hai. Lantern par halki chheentein theek hain lekin isay barish mein bahar na chhorein.
