# Privacy notice (summary)

Noorbagh collects the name, phone number, delivery address, and order history of customers to deliver orders and provide support. Card details are handled by the payment provider; Noorbagh does not store full card numbers.

Customer data is not sold to other companies. It is shared only with couriers and payment providers as needed for an order.

Customers can ask for a copy of their data or for deletion by emailing privacy@noorbagh.example. Requests are answered within 30 days.
