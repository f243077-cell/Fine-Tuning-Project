# Employee leave policy

Full-time employees get 20 days of annual leave, 10 days of sick leave, and the public holidays announced by the government. Annual leave can be carried over up to 10 days into the next year.

Maternity leave is 16 weeks with full pay. Paternity leave is 10 days with full pay.

Leave requests are made in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance, except sick leave, which can be reported on the same day.
