# Cash on delivery aur payment

Noorbagh card, bank transfer, mobile wallet aur cash on delivery qubool karta hai. Cash on delivery Rs. 50,000 tak ke order par milti hai. Is se bara order pehle card ya bank transfer se pay karna hota hai.

Rs. 20,000 se zyada ke order par partner banks ke zariye 3, 6 ya 12 mahine ki qiston ki suhulat hai. Qiston par mark-up aap ka bank tay karta hai, Noorbagh apni taraf se koi fee nahi leta.
