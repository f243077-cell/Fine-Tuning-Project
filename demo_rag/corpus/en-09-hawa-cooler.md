# Product sheet: Hawa air cooler AC-40

The Hawa AC-40 is an evaporative air cooler with a 40-litre water tank. It cools rooms of up to 250 square feet and uses 180 watts. It has three fan speeds, a swing mode, and a timer of up to 8 hours.

The cooling pads should be cleaned every two weeks in summer and replaced once a year. Using it with a closed window reduces cooling, because evaporative coolers need fresh air.

The list price is Rs. 32,000. The pump and motor have a 1-year warranty. Delivery is a fixed Rs. 600.
