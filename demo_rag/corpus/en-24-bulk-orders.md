# Bulk and business orders

Businesses, schools, and offices that order more than 20 units of a product get a bulk discount: 8% for 20 to 99 units and 12% for 100 units or more.

Bulk orders are quoted by the business sales team within 2 working days. Payment is by bank transfer; 30% is paid in advance and the rest on delivery.

Bulk customers get a dedicated account manager and can ask for on-site installation of fans.
