## Other release items

- **Novelty claim re-checked (PRD section 2).** Searches on 2026-11-16 found Roman Urdu only in a benchmark paper (RUAB), in two small rule and TF-IDF course projects, and in Urdu-script models; the README states a narrow claim and says it is not a claim to be first. See the "Related work" section of the README.
- **Licences.** The three Qwen models are Apache-2.0 (checked on their Hugging Face pages). The training data has CC BY-SA 4.0 rows. Code: Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`); released weights: CC BY-SA 4.0 with the Qwen Apache-2.0 notice kept (D59).
- **Human review still open (not blocking the release, but the README says so):** the 80 seeds, the 300-row test set, the Roman Urdu corpus, attack scenarios, golden questions and playground samples were drafted by the AI assistant (D22, D53, D54).
